Questions

Before you book.

Do I need to already use QuickBooks Online?

Yes, we work in QuickBooks Online on your own subscription. If you are on something else, or starting from spreadsheets, we can set up QuickBooks Online for you for a one-time $300–$500.

What if my books are months behind?

That is common. We quote a flat one-time cleanup fee based on how far behind you are and the condition of the records, then move you into monthly service once you are current.

Who will I actually talk to?

One dedicated contact, from your first call through every monthly report. They know your business, answer your questions, and send your reports. Other people do the production work behind them, so your books keep moving if someone is out.

What access do you need to my accounts?

You invite us to your QuickBooks Online file as your accountant, and we work from your connected bank and card feeds. You never email passwords.

What happens if I go over my transaction limit?

Extra transactions are +$75 a month per 100 over your plan limit. If you go over regularly, we will suggest the plan that fits.

Do you file my taxes?

No. We keep your books accurate and current, and your CPA or tax preparer uses them at tax time. If you do not have one, we can talk about what to look for.
