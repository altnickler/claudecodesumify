FAQ

Before you book

What exactly do you do each month?

We categorize your transactions, reconcile your bank and credit card accounts, review the work a second time, and send your Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet with a plain-English summary within 10 business days of month-end. Standard and Growth add basic AR/AP tracking and a monthly check-in call.

Do I need to already use QuickBooks Online?

We work in QuickBooks Online, on your own subscription. If you're on something else or starting from spreadsheets, we can set up QuickBooks Online for you for a one-time $300–$500.

What if my books are months behind?

That's common. We quote a flat one-time cleanup fee based on how far behind you are and the condition of your records, then move you into monthly service once you're current. More on catch-up bookkeeping.

Who will I actually talk to?

One dedicated contact, from your first call through every monthly report. They know your business, answer your questions, and send your reports. Other people do the production work behind them, so your books keep moving if someone is out.

What access do you need to my accounts?

You invite us to your QuickBooks Online file as your accountant, and we work from your connected bank and card feeds. You never email passwords.

What happens if I go over my transaction limit?

Extra transactions are +$75 a month per 100 over your plan limit. If you go over regularly, we'll suggest the plan that fits.

Do you file taxes or run payroll?

No. We keep your books accurate and current, and your CPA or tax preparer uses them at tax time. If you don't have one, we can talk about what to look for. Payroll support is available as an add-on (+$15/month per employee), but we don't run payroll or file payroll taxes.

What happens after the call?

If you want to go ahead, you get a short engagement letter to sign and a link to set up payment. Then we walk you through giving us access to QuickBooks Online.
