Built for Owner-Operators Who Mean Business.

  • You regularly manage fuel, repairs, insurance, and settlement transactions.

  • You're comfortable investing at least $500/month in professional bookkeeping.

Not Every Trucking Business Needs Us.

Our ongoing bookkeeping service may not be the right fit for every situation.

  • You're a W-2 company driver rather than a trucking business owner.
  • You're still planning to purchase your first truck and haven't started operating.
  • You only need personal tax preparation or an annual tax return.
  • You're looking exclusively for a one-time bookkeeping cleanup with no ongoing service.
  • You prefer handling your books yourself using free or very low-cost software.
  • You're not currently prepared to invest $500 or more per month in ongoing bookkeeping.

These criteria apply to this ongoing monthly bookkeeping offer. If your books are behind, we can quote a separate cleanup before monthly service starts.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does bookkeeping cost?

Sumify's ongoing bookkeeping starts at $500/month. Your final monthly rate depends on transaction volume, number of accounts, and service requirements. We'll discuss the appropriate plan during your free discovery call.

Can you handle fuel purchases, repairs, and settlement deposits?

Yes. As part of our monthly bookkeeping service, we can categorize and reconcile ordinary trucking business transactions, including fuel purchases, maintenance expenses, insurance payments, and settlement deposits, using the records available in QuickBooks Online. Specialized transportation compliance and tax filings are separate from this service.

Do I need QuickBooks Online?

Sumify performs its bookkeeping services using QuickBooks Online. If you aren't currently using it, we can discuss setup options during your consultation. Setup may involve an additional fee.

What if my trucking business's books are already behind?

We can evaluate your existing records and quote a separate cleanup project before transitioning you into ongoing monthly bookkeeping.

Do you prepare taxes or handle IFTA filings?

No. This offer covers ongoing bookkeeping, transaction categorization, reconciliations, and monthly financial reporting. Tax preparation, IFTA filing, and transportation compliance services are not included.

What will I receive every month?

You receive ongoing transaction categorization, bank and credit card reconciliation, and monthly Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet reports according to your service plan.

What happens during the free consultation?

We'll discuss how many trucks you operate, how you currently manage your bookkeeping, your monthly transaction volume, and what services you need. We'll determine whether Sumify is a good fit and explain the expected pricing and next steps.
