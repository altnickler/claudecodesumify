FAQ

Catch-up questions

How is the catch-up fee decided?

It's a flat one-time fee based on how far behind you are and the condition of your records. The table above shows typical ranges, and you'll get your exact quote before any work starts.

Do I have to sign up for monthly bookkeeping too?

Catch-up is designed to lead into monthly service, so your books stay current once they're clean. Monthly plans start at $500/month. We'll talk through what makes sense for you on the call.

I'm not on QuickBooks Online. Can you still help?

We work in QuickBooks Online, on your own subscription. If you're on something else or starting from spreadsheets, we can set up QuickBooks Online for you for a one-time $300–$500.

Will you file my back taxes?

No. We bring your books current so your CPA or tax preparer can work from accurate records. If you don't have one, we can talk about what to look for.

What access do you need?

You invite us to your QuickBooks Online file as your accountant, and we work from your connected bank and card feeds and statements. You never email passwords.
