Privacy policy

How we handle your information.

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Sumify LLC ("Sumify", "we"). We provide monthly bookkeeping services for small businesses. You can reach us at info@getsumify.com.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. When you book a call, you give us your name, email address, and a few details about your business, such as your business type and how far behind your books are.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we use cookies and similar tools that record how you reached this site and which pages you viewed.

How we use it

  • To schedule and hold your call and to follow up with you about it.
  • To understand which of our ads and pages lead people to book, so we can improve them.
  • To provide our services, if you become a client. The financial records you share with us as a client are covered by your engagement agreement.

Who we share it with

We use service providers to run our business, including a scheduling tool, email, and website hosting, and advertising and analytics platforms such as Google and Meta. They process information on our behalf. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Cookies and ad choices

We use advertising and analytics tools that may set cookies. You can manage ad personalization through your Google ad settings and your Meta ad preferences, and you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep booking information for as long as we need it to follow up with you and to meet our legal and business obligations, then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing info@getsumify.com. Some states give residents additional privacy rights, and we will honor those requests as the law requires.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

This site is for business owners and is not directed to anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date above.
